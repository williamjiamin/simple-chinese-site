How to read this sheet

The spoken-tone switch starts on. Press the button, or focus it and press Space or Enter, to show dictionary tones in the large line. The smaller dictionary line remains visible.

Boundaries matter

Each example supplies its own syllables, dictionary tones and grouping. The tool does not guess the reading of unfamiliar characters or infer sentence structure.

Without scripts

All text remains readable when scripts are unavailable. A display compiled with the kill switch active contains the unmodified template instead of added study cards.
