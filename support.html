Common questions

Why does tone feedback say it "couldn't hear me"?

The app refuses to judge recordings that are too quiet, too short, clipped, or noisy — that protects you from wrong "your tone is off" messages. Try a quieter spot, hold the phone closer, and speak in full voice. You can always skip a speaking step and practise it later from Review.

Does the app work offline?

Yes — all core lessons, writing practice, and reviews work fully offline. Purchases and restore need a connection.

How do I restore purchases on a new device?

Settings → Purchases → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple account.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Privacy & your data → Erase all my data. Deleting the app does the same. There is nothing to delete on our side — we hold no copy.
