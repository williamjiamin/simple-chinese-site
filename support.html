Common questions

Is the narration a human recording?

No. Version 1.0 uses bundled neural narration with Apple on-device speech fallback as a general listening aid and labels it in the lesson. Interactive tone diagrams and authored Pinyin provide the learning guidance; the narration is not presented as native-speaker reference audio.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The complete version 1.0 course, device narration, writing practice, quizzes, and reviews work offline after the app is installed. Optional Explore pictures and linked online dictionary entries need an internet connection.

Does version 1.0 include purchases or subscriptions?

No. Every included lesson is free. There is no paywall, subscription, in-app purchase, or restore-purchases control.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Privacy & your data → Erase all my data clears learner data and the app’s public-content caches. Deleting the app removes local app data, subject to device backups. This does not establish deletion of Cloudflare operational data from optional Explore requests. See our privacy policy, or contact us about service-data questions.
