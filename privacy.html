Privacy Policy

Simple Chinese: Pinyin & Hanzi · Effective 2026-07-27

The short version

We collect no data from the app. There is no account, analytics service, advertising, or tracking. Your learning progress stays on your device. Version 1.0 contains no speaking or recording lesson.

Data the app stores on your device only

  • Learning progress: lesson completion, review scheduling, mastery scores, streaks, and settings.
  • Writing results: stroke counts, hints, and accuracy, but not the coordinates you draw.

This data never leaves your device via the app. You can erase all of it at any time in Settings → Privacy → "Erase all my data". Deleting the app also deletes it (subject to your own device backups, which you control).

Audio and network access

  • Version 1.0 does not ask you to record your voice and does not use speech recognition in its shipped lessons.
  • General Chinese narration is generated on your device by Apple's speech synthesizer. The app does not send your learning activity or personal data to generate it.
  • Version 1.0 has no developer content server enabled. Its complete course is bundled with the app and works offline after installation.

Purchases

Version 1.0 has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, paid products, or payment-data handling. Downloading the app is handled by Apple under your Apple account and Apple's terms.

Children

The app is a general-audience product. It collects no data from anyone, including children. The optional parent-guided mode changes presentation only.

What we would tell you about changes

If a future version enables speaking practice, downloadable reviewed media, cloud features, or any other new data flow, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be reviewed and updated before that feature is activated.
