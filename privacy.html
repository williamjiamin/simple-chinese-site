The short version

We collect no personal data. There is no account, no analytics service, no advertising, and no tracking. Your learning progress stays on your device. Your voice is processed on your device and immediately discarded.

Data the app stores on your device only

  • Learning progress: lesson completion, review scheduling, mastery scores, streaks, and settings.
  • Purchase state, as reported by Apple's StoreKit.

This data never leaves your device via the app. You can erase all of it at any time in Settings → Privacy → "Erase all my data". Deleting the app also deletes it (subject to your own device backups, which you control).

Your voice

  • Pronunciation practice records short clips in memory, analyses them on the device, and discards them. The app never saves, stores, or uploads recordings.
  • Tone feedback (pitch analysis) is computed entirely on the device.
  • Word checking uses Apple's speech recognition. The app requests on-device recognition whenever your device supports it. If on-device recognition is unavailable, Apple's speech service may process the audio under Apple's privacy terms; the app itself transmits nothing.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled by Apple. The app never sees your payment details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings.

Children

The app is a general-audience product. It collects no data from anyone, including children. The optional parent-guided mode changes presentation only.

What we would tell you about changes

If a future version ever changes what data is handled (for example, an optional cloud backup), this policy will be updated first and the change will be opt-in, clearly explained inside the app.
