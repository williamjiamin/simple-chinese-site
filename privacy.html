Privacy Policy

Simple Chinese: Pinyin & Hanzi · Effective 2026-10-12

Learning data stays on your device

Simple Chinese: Pinyin & Hanzi stores your learning progress and settings on your device. Its bundled course works offline after installation. You do not need an account. This release contains no advertising or tracking SDK.

Local data includes lesson completion, review scheduling, mastery scores, streaks, settings and writing results. The app does not send this learning data to the optional Explore service.

On-device narration

Chinese narration is generated on your device using bundled neural speech or Apple's speech synthesizer. English playback uses the available device speech voices. Version 1.0 has no speaking or recording lesson and does not ask you to record your voice. Voice audio is not uploaded to generate lesson narration.

Optional online Explore

Opening Explore can request a public scene catalog, descriptions, pictures and explicitly linked dictionary entries from dict.simplelits.com. Requests identify the public content being loaded. They do not contain your lesson progress, voice recordings or arbitrary text entered into local Chinese lookup. The service uses Cloudflare Workers and R2 infrastructure.

Cloudflare processes ordinary connection and HTTP request metadata to deliver and protect the service. This can include your IP address, requested URL, user-agent information and approximate location derived from network information. Our optional Worker request logs, traces and issues, Logpush exports and R2 Data Access Logs were disabled when checked on 11 October 2026. This does not disable Cloudflare's own network and security processing.

Cloudflare documents up to 31 days of history for Free-plan Security Analytics and Security Events. That history window is not a promise that every category of provider data is deleted after 31 days. Cloudflare describes its processing and retention in its privacy policy. Contact us below for service-data questions or privacy requests.

Deleting local data

The app caches downloaded public content on your device. Settings → Privacy → “Erase all my data” clears learner data and the app's public-content caches. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to device backups you control. Erasing device data does not establish deletion of provider operational data.

Apple services and purchases

Version 1.0 has no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Apple's download and optional system-diagnostic services are governed by your Apple settings and Apple's policies. The app has no custom crash-reporting service.

General audience and updates

The app is for a general audience. Parent-guided presentation does not add an account or send learning progress to a server. If features or data practices change, we update this notice and the App Store privacy information.
