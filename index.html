Simple Chinese: Pīnyīn & Hànzì

Sound → pinyin → tone → meaning → character → word → sentence. One connected path, for iPhone and iPad.

Privacy Terms of Use Support

See and hear how Mandarin works

Start with a seven-day Pinyin bootcamp, interactive tone contours, and clearly labelled on-device Chinese narration. Authored Pinyin and tone guidance connect each sound to the words and characters you are learning.

Write characters the right way

Stroke-order animation, guided tracing, then writing from memory — three assistance levels that fade as you improve, powered by real stroke data. Works with your finger or Apple Pencil.

A curriculum, not a card deck

Follow 534 structured lessons across HSK levels 1–6, with more than 5,000 reading, vocabulary, sentence-building, quiz, and writing exercises. The seven-day Pinyin bootcamp never locks you out for missing a day.

Private by design

No account. No ads. No tracking. Version 1.0 contains no speaking or recording lessons, and learning progress lives only on your device. Every included lesson is free and works offline after installation.
