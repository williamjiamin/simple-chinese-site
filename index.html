Simple Chinese: Pīnyín & Hànzì

Sound → pinyin → tone → meaning → character → word → sentence. One connected path, for iPhone and iPad.

Privacy Terms of Use Support

Hear your tones, not just guesses

Record yourself and see your actual pitch curve next to the target tone shape. When the recording is too quiet or noisy to judge, the app says so honestly — it never blames your tone for a bad microphone moment.

Write characters the right way

Stroke-order animation, guided tracing, then writing from memory — three assistance levels that fade as you improve, powered by real stroke data. Works with your finger or Apple Pencil.

A curriculum, not a card deck

Pinyin foundations, tone pairs, high-frequency characters aligned with HSK levels, culture notes that explain why the language works — plus a 7-day pinyin bootcamp that never locks you out for missing a day.

Private by design

No account. No ads. No tracking. Your voice is analysed on the device and immediately discarded — recordings are never saved or uploaded by the app. Learning progress lives only on your device.
